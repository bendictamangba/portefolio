E-gbalè

Centralized health records and online appointments

A platform that brings health records and appointment booking together in one place, on both the frontend and the backend.

Demo on request →

01

The starting point

A person’s health information and appointment booking often live in two different tools. E-gbalè brings them together in a single platform, with one simple idea: find your records and book a slot without switching places.

02

How I built it

I developed the platform end to end with Next.js, using the framework for both the interface and the backend. This single-stack choice shortens the distance between the two sides and keeps a consistent structure from the first screen down to the business logic.

03

What it changes

A platform where health records are centralized and appointments are booked online — a complete journey, with no break between reviewing information and making a reservation.

04

Key features

  • Centralized health records
  • Online appointment booking
  • Interface and logic in a single Next.js stack
